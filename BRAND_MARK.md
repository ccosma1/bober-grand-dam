# Bober Grand Dam mark

Metaphor: **the crest road between the reservoir and the spillway.** Teal water is held above the bank and drops through one chute into the ink plunge pool. The amber crest is the race line, climbing to the right. A chunky cedar bowl — flat rim, solid hull, no face — rides that line. Mission: "Race the bank. Drift the dam. First to the crest wins." At 32px the read is **amber crest, teal chute, ink pool**, with the bowl as a dark hull on the road.

Shape: **full-bleed square section** of the dam. The silhouette is the structure itself, edge to edge — not an object floating in a tile. Teal reservoir bleeds off the top. Crest amber is a thick band whose top rises toward the right finish. Warm concrete is the downstream face. One spillway bay cuts the road from the top edge into the pool and widens slightly at the toe. The bowl is a solid trapezoid planted on the left crest, wider at the rim than the keel, tilted with the road. Its hull stands above the bank (against the reservoir) and its keel bites the amber, so it reads as the racer, not a slot in the road. No sky, no crescent, no stroke, no rounded-square app tile, no inner glow circle.
