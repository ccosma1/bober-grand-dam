GRAND DAM

Race the bank. Drift the dam. First to the crest wins.

Local race. Three laps. You and three racers.

Fan game by a holder. Unofficial — not the project team.

Keys: A D steer · W go · S brake · Shift drift · F fire · arrows + Enter

HOW TO PLAY

On a phone, push the stick to steer and to go. FIRE is on the right. A laptop uses the keys.

Hold a turn. Let go when the bands spark.

Boxes sit on the road. Drive through one to hold a throw.

FIRE uses it. There is a short wait before the next throw.

Blue Lodge Orb only while you are 1st or 2nd.

Boxes also hold Thunder Twig, Log Roller, Mirror Mist, and Crest Bomb.

A D or arrows steer. W or up goes. S or down brakes. Shift or Space drifts. F, E, or Enter fires. Arrows and Enter run the menus.
