BOBER
GRAND DAM

Race the bank. Boost the dam. First to the crest wins.

Local race. You and three racers. Dam, Frost, and Crown are four laps. Oasis and Sky are three.

Fan game by a holder. Unofficial — not the project team.

Pick a track

Pick a beaver

Keys: A D steer · W go · S brake · F fire · arrows + Enter

HOW TO PLAY

On a phone, push the stick to steer and to go. FIRE is on the right. A laptop shows steer, go, brake, and FIRE on the screen, and the keys still work.

Boxes sit on the road. Drive through one to hold a throw. The crest shows its name.

FIRE uses it. BOOST is a short amber burst of speed. There is a short wait before the next throw.

The box also holds Stick Trap, Pinecone Barrage, Dam Surge, Lodge Magnet, Bark Buckler, Meteor Chip, and Resin Slick.

A D or arrows steer. W or up goes. S or down brakes. F, E, or Enter fires. Arrows and Enter run the menus.
